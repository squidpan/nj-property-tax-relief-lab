# Official Source Registry

This note is the human-readable companion to the future machine-readable source registry under `data/sources/`.

## Initial source families

The structured registry should include current official New Jersey sources for:

- master property-tax-relief information;
- ANCHOR eligibility;
- ANCHOR benefit calculation/payment timing;
- Senior Freeze eligibility;
- Senior Freeze benefit calculation/payment timing;
- Stay NJ eligibility and current benefit schedule;
- property-tax-relief FAQ;
- PAS-1 application/instructions.

## Important known conflict to preserve

Earlier PAS-1 instructions contained a Stay NJ income figure that was explicitly subject to subsequent FY2027 budget action. Current post-budget Stay NJ program guidance must be treated as the current operational rule after verification.

Do not encode the superseded figure as an executable current rule.
